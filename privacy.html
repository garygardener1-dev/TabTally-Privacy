Privacy Policy for TabTally

Effective Date: November 15, 2026

TabTally ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is stored, accessed, and protected when you use the TabTally mobile application.

1. Local Financial Data Storage

All bills, income schedules, categories, notes, and payment records you enter into TabTally are stored locally on your device. We do not operate private backend servers and we never collect, view, or sell your personal financial entries.

2. Optional Google Drive Backup

If you choose to sign in with your Google Account to enable cloud backups, TabTally uses the restricted Google Drive drive.appdata scope. This permission allows TabTally to create, read, and delete only its own backup file (tabtally-backup.json) inside a hidden, app-specific folder on your personal Google Drive. TabTally cannot view, access, or modify any other files in your Google Drive. You can disconnect your account or delete your backup data at any time from the app's Settings screen or your Google Account permissions page.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of TabTally displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address, and general interaction data to provide relevant ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud. Users who purchase the one-time "Remove Ads" upgrade have all advertisements disabled.

4. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional one-time upgrades are processed securely through Google Play Billing. We do not collect or store your credit card numbers or banking details; we only receive confirmation from Google Play of whether your account holds an active entitlement for the ad-free upgrade.

5. Children's Privacy

TabTally is not directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Terms of Use Disclaimer

TabTally is provided "as is" as a personal organizational and reminder tool. While we strive for accurate date and balance calculations, you remain responsible for verifying your own bill due dates, payment amounts, and financial obligations to avoid late fees or overdrafts.

7. Contact & Data Deletion Requests

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to request assistance deleting your local or Google Drive backup data, please contact us at: tabtally.studio@gmail.com

Account & Data Deletion

TabTally does not store user accounts or financial data on developer servers. All app data is stored locally on your device, and any optional cloud backup is stored exclusively inside your own personal Google Drive (appDataFolder).

  • Delete Local Data: Open TabTally → Settings → tap Reset all data, or uninstall the app from your device.
  • Disconnect Google Account & Delete Cloud Backup: Open TabTally → Settings → tap Sign Out, and remove TabTally's access anytime in your Google Account permissions (Google Account → Security → Third-party apps with account access) or email our developer support address listed on the Google Play Store page to request assistance.
